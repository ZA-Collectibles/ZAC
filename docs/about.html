Operating history

A history before the website.

ZA Collectibles has existed through years of marketplace activity, including Bid or Buy and Bob Shop, while the people around it bring more than four decades of combined relevant experience across collecting and specialist fields.

Why this house exists

Because objects become more interesting when someone cares enough to look properly.

Some clients want a valuation. Others want to sell. Some are trying to find the last piece in a set. Some inherited something they do not understand. Some simply want to know what they are holding.

ZA Collectibles is designed to be useful across all of those moments.
