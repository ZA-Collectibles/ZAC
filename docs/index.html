LOOK CLOSER / TEST YOUR EYE

Real or reproduction?

Collectors learn to distrust the obvious view. Marks, wear, edges, tooling and context can matter more than the first impression. Start with the detail.

Would you call it genuine from this image alone?

Make the call, then reveal the collecting lesson.

The strongest answer is often the least theatrical one: not enough evidence yet. A macro detail can reveal useful clues, but responsible attribution depends on the whole object, construction, provenance and context. Knowing what not to conclude is part of knowing what to look for.
